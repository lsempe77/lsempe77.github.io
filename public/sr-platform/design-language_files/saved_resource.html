Impact of school feeding programmes on enrolment, attendance and cognition among primary-school children in sub-Saharan Africa

Okeke, A., Adeyemi, F. & Mwangi, J. · 2019 · Journal of Development Effectiveness

A cluster-randomised trial across 128 government primary schools evaluated a daily in-school meal against usual provision. After two academic years the programme raised enrolment by 0.34 SD and attendance by 6.1 percentage points; effects on a standardised cognition battery were positive but imprecise.
