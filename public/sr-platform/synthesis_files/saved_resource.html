⚠ contradiction guard — flagged before drafting

Mensah 2021 reports an effect in the opposite direction to the pooled estimate (−0.11 vs. +0.26). This was detected by a deterministic sign comparison, not the language model, and is passed into the narrative prompt by name — the model is required to address it, not permitted to omit it.

SWiM narrative — AI-drafted draft for confirmation, not an autonomous conclusion

Four of five studies found a positive effect of school feeding on enrolment, and the pooled estimate (SMD 0.26, 95% CI 0.08–0.44) supports a small-to-moderate benefit. One study (Mensah 2021) found a small negative, imprecise effect — plausibly explained by its quasi-experimental design and shorter follow-up period rather than a genuine contrary finding…

B

Why the guard runs first, not after

a language model is never the last line of defence against smoothing over evidence
Pure and deterministic
The sign comparison is ordinary code, not a model call — it produces the same flagged study list every time, on every re-run, with nothing to calibrate or hallucinate.
Named, not summarised away
Flagged study IDs are injected into the narrative prompt by name. A model asked to explain a specific contradiction behaves very differently from one asked to "summarise the findings."
Confirmed effects only
Pooling reads from the extraction confirmation gate — an AI-read sample size or effect size nobody has checked can never silently enter this forest plot.
A draft, cached and reproducible
The narrative is cached against the exact pool run, model and seed that produced it — re-running synthesis with the same inputs reproduces the same draft, not a new one each time.
