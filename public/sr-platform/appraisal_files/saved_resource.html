Why seven tools instead of one

the instrument follows the study design, not the other way round
Pluggable, catalogue-registered
RoB 2ROBINS-IROBINS-EQUADAS-2AMSTAR-2JBI CohortNewcastle-Ottawa
Activating a tool for a study generates its extraction form straight from the catalogue — RCTs get RoB 2, non-randomised studies get ROBINS-I, diagnostic-accuracy studies get QUADAS-2.
Same conflict machinery, reused
A second reviewer's RoB judgement runs through the same dual-reviewer conflict engine extraction already has — a disagreement on D2 is a resolvable row, not a silent overwrite.
Summary-of-findings, generated
The five GRADE factors and the pooled effect combine into a SoF table, exportable to CSV/NDJSON — never hand-assembled from five separate documents.
AI suggests, never finalises
An AI-suggested domain judgement lands unconfirmed, same gate as extraction. A GRADE rating with no human confirmation cannot enter a report.

This is a static direction for the appraisal screens, not wired code. The traffic-light domain row and the confirmation gate are shared with the Design Language artifact; the severity-selector row and the certainty badge are the new patterns this screen contributes.
